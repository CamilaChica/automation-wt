# Terms of Service Draft

**Legal review required before publication.** This draft identifies commercial terms that must be approved before public launch.

Before publication, define:

- Service scope and availability commitments
- Quote validity and expiration rules
- Pricing, taxes, freight, payment, and invoicing terms
- Purchase-order acceptance and cancellation rules
- Inspection, returns, warranty, and certificate responsibilities
- Export-control and denied-party obligations
- Customer acceptable-use and security responsibilities
- Liability limits, dispute venue, and governing law
- Support channels and termination rights
