# Privacy Policy Draft

**Legal review required before publication.** This draft identifies topics the operator must finalize before public launch.

Winged Tycoons processes account information, contact details, RFQ content, supplier communications, quote data, purchase-order information, shipment data, audit records, and authentication events to operate the procurement service.

Before publication, specify:

- Legal entity and contact address
- Purposes and lawful bases for processing
- Service providers and international transfers
- Retention periods for RFQs, mail, quotes, audit logs, and security events
- Customer access, correction, deletion, and export procedures
- Cookie and session-token disclosures
- Security incident notification process
- ITAR/EAR and export-control data handling restrictions
- Data-subject and business-customer request channels
