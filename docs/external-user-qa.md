# External User Q&A

## 1. What is this portal for?

This portal is the customer-facing entry point for Winged Tycoons procurement services. Customers can search the customer-safe catalog, submit a request for quote (RFQ), contact the team, submit required purchase-order documents for supported quotes, and look up shipment status when they have a tracking token.

## 2. How do I submit a request?

Open the customer portal at https://winged-tycoons-frontend.onrender.com/customer-portal, sign in, and enter the request details, including:

- part number or description
- quantity
- urgency level
- delivery location
- company and contact email
- any additional requirements or supporting parts list

Accept the required agreement before submitting. The request is routed to the internal team for review. You can also contact the team from the portal using the Contact Us menu or email parts@wingedtycoons.com.

## 3. Do I need to create an account?

Customer access uses email one-time-password (OTP) sign-in. Enter an email address you can access and use the code sent to that address. If the verification email does not arrive, check spam and contact parts@wingedtycoons.com. Local development may show a test code; that is not production email delivery and should never be used as evidence that a production account was verified.

## 4. What happens after I submit an RFQ?

After submission, the intake service acknowledges the request and it may proceed through inventory, supplier, compliance, and internal review. The workflow may:

- find a matching internal stock item
- request supplier pricing
- flag a compliance concern
- create a quote draft for internal review
- return a follow-up request for clarification

A submission acknowledgement or request ID confirms only that intake accepted the request. It does not confirm that a quote was created, approved, or emailed. If you have not received a response, contact the team with your request ID.

## 5. How long does a quote take?

Timing depends on part availability, supplier response, compliance documentation, and internal review. No quote-time guarantee is stated here. Contact the team with your request ID if you need a confirmed update.

## 6. What if my requested part is not in stock?

The team may search alternate suppliers. If a part is unavailable or needs special certification or traceability, the team may request more information. Supplier outreach is not a confirmed quote or delivery commitment.

## 7. Can I edit my request after submitting it?

The portal does not guarantee that an already submitted RFQ can be edited. Contact parts@wingedtycoons.com with the request ID and the correction you need. Changes may require a new review.

## 8. What does a quote status mean?

Internal processing can include states such as:

- received
- parsed
- inventory checked
- sourcing in progress
- compliance review
- pricing review
- approval pending
- quote sent
- rejected

These are operational workflow stages and may not all be displayed in the customer portal. A pending request means processing may still be underway; contact the team for a confirmed update. A displayed status alone does not prove that an email was delivered.

## 9. Is my information secure?

The portal uses OTP authentication and separates customer access from internal operations. Avoid submitting sensitive or export-controlled information unless you are authorized and the team has provided an approved channel for it. Do not share OTP codes or account sessions.

## 10. What if there is a problem with my request?

Contact parts@wingedtycoons.com and include your RFQ ID, company, and the issue. The team may need more information about traceability, condition, or alternate sourcing. If the portal reports an intake failure, submitting repeatedly may create duplicate requests; contact the team first.

## 11. What should I expect from communications?

Depending on processing and configured services, the team may send:

- an acknowledgement of the RFQ
- a request for clarification
- a finalized quote after internal review
- a rejection or partial-availability response
- shipment or delivery follow-up communication

An acknowledgement is not a finalized quote. Treat a quote as issued only when you have received it through an official Winged Tycoons communication channel.

## 12. What if I need a quote for a part with special compliance requirements?

Let the team know the required condition, certificates, and traceability documents. These requests require additional review and may take longer; the team may ask you for clarification.

## 13. Can I access internal pricing or supplier data?

No. The customer-safe catalog does not provide internal cost, serial-number, warehouse-location, or supplier contact details. Availability shown in search is informational and should be confirmed in a formal quote.

## 14. What do I do if I get an error or the portal does not load?

Check the following:

- the portal URL is correct
- your browser session is active
- your OTP code is current and entered correctly
- you are using the correct portal route
- the portal displays no service error

If an error persists, contact parts@wingedtycoons.com. Do not send passwords, OTP codes, or payment credentials in email.

## 15. Who should I contact for help?

Use the portal's Contact Us menu or email parts@wingedtycoons.com. Include your RFQ ID where available, but do not include OTP codes or secrets.

## 16. How do I submit a purchase order?

For a supported quote, enter the quote ID, PO number, and customer email, then upload the signed export certification, signed KYC form, and purchase-order document. The portal requires all three documents before it submits the PO. If you receive an error, contact the team before resubmitting so they can check whether it was already received.

## 17. How do I track a shipment?

Use the shipment tracking form with the tracking token provided for your shipment. Sample routes or sample dashboard telemetry are not live tracking results. If you do not have a tracking token or tracking lookup fails, contact parts@wingedtycoons.com.

## 18. Which portal languages are available?

The customer portal supports English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Dutch, Arabic, and Hindi. Arabic uses a right-to-left layout. Choose a language with the globe control; a saved preference takes precedence on return visits.
